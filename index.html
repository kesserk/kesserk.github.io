T2D Risk Predictor

In progress

A fairness-audited Type 2 Diabetes risk model on open NHANES data, mirroring the method of my doctoral research. It compares logistic regression, XGBoost, an MLP and a tabular transformer on discrimination and calibration, overall and per subgroup. The FastAPI service, Docker image, CI, exploratory analysis and baseline model are in place; the subgroup audit and SHAP explanations are next.

Diabetes Guidance RAG

Design stage

A retrieval-augmented question answering system over NICE NG28 and the ADA Standards of Care. The design is evaluation-first: hybrid BM25 and PubMedBERT retrieval with reranking, answers that cite the guideline section they came from, and a measured hallucination rate instead of an assumed one.

  • LangChain
  • pgvector
  • PubMedBERT
  • RAGAS
  • Langfuse
  • FastAPI
Read the design on GitHub
