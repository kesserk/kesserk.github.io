My work is on clinical risk models, and mostly on one question: does the model work as well for every group of patients as it does on average? What I'm building

T2D Risk Predictor

Question
Is a diabetes risk model equally well calibrated across ethnicity, sex and age bands? Where it isn't, how much of the gap can be fixed?
Data
NHANES, which is open. The NHS data from my PhD can't be shared, so this repeats the method in public.
Method
Logistic regression, XGBoost, an MLP and a tabular transformer, compared on AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score and calibration error. Fairlearn for the subgroup audit, SHAP for explanations, served with FastAPI in Docker.
Status
In progress. The API skeleton, Docker image, CI, exploratory analysis and a baseline model are done. The audit and explanations are not, and I won't post numbers until they are. NHANES has few South Asian participants, so the results will need careful reading.

Diabetes Guidance RAG

Question
How often does a question answering system over clinical guidelines make things up, and can you measure it properly?
Data
NICE NG28 and the ADA Standards of Care.
Method
BM25 plus PubMedBERT embeddings in pgvector, with reranking. Answers cite the guideline section they came from. Evaluation is RAGAS alongside a hand-written set of clinical questions.
Status
Design only. The repository holds the plan and a checklist with nothing ticked yet.
