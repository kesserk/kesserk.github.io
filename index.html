I've taught Computer Science for over ten years and I'm now moving into machine learning engineering. I'm looking for ML Engineer, AI Engineer or Applied Scientist roles in the UAE (on site or hybrid) or remote.

I'm doing a part-time PhD at the University of Portsmouth on predicting Type 2 Diabetes risk early in South Asian populations. The data comes from Connected Bradford and UK Biobank.

A risk model can look fine on average and still be wrong for one group of patients. Most diabetes risk scores were built on mainly white European data, and South Asians get Type 2 Diabetes at two to four times the rate. That matters here in the Gulf as well, where diabetes rates are among the highest in the world. A lot of my time goes on calibration and on checking results group by group.

I teach full time in Abu Dhabi and do the PhD and these projects alongside it.

Projects

T2D Risk Predictor

A Type 2 Diabetes risk model that I check by ethnicity, sex and age band as well as overall.

Data
NHANES, which is public. I can't share the NHS data from my PhD, so this uses the same method on open data.
Method
Four models: logistic regression, XGBoost, an MLP and a tabular transformer. I compare them on AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score and calibration error. Fairlearn for the group checks, SHAP for explanations, FastAPI and Docker for serving.
Status
Early. The API skeleton, Docker, CI, the EDA notebook and a baseline model are done. The fairness audit and the SHAP work aren't, so I have no results to show yet. NHANES also doesn't have many South Asian participants, which will limit what the results can say.

Diabetes Guidance RAG

Question answering over the NICE NG28 and ADA diabetes guidelines. I mainly want to measure how often it gets things wrong or makes things up.

Method
BM25 and PubMedBERT embeddings in pgvector, with a reranker. Answers point back to the guideline section they used. I'll test it with RAGAS and a set of clinical questions I write by hand.
Status
Not built yet. The repo has the design and the to-do list.
