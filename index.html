About

I'm doing a part-time PhD at the University of Portsmouth on predicting Type 2 Diabetes risk early in South Asian populations. The data comes from Connected Bradford and UK Biobank.

A risk model can look fine on average and still be wrong for one group of patients. Most diabetes risk scores were built on mainly white European data, and South Asians get Type 2 Diabetes at two to four times the rate.

That matters here in the Gulf as well, where diabetes rates are among the highest in the world. A lot of my time goes on calibration and on checking results group by group.

I teach full time in Abu Dhabi and do the PhD and these projects alongside it.

T2D Risk Predictor

Early stage

A Type 2 Diabetes risk model that I check by ethnicity, sex and age band as well as overall.

Data
NHANES, which is public. I can't share the NHS data from my PhD, so this uses the same method on open data.
Method
Four models: logistic regression, XGBoost, an MLP and a tabular transformer. I compare them on AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score and calibration error.
Status
The API skeleton, Docker, CI, the EDA notebook and a baseline model are done. The fairness audit and the SHAP work aren't, so I have no results to show yet. NHANES also doesn't have many South Asian participants, which will limit what the results can say.
