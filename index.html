FutureTech is a tutoring platform I founded for parents in the UAE and the UK. Harbour will independently test, monitor and certify AI products used by children, and is launching soon. In progress

A work-in-progress pipeline for Type-2 diabetes risk prediction on the open NHANES dataset, alongside the Portsmouth PhD. NHS data from the doctorate is not in this repository. No results are published yet.

In the repository today: a FastAPI skeleton, Docker, GitHub Actions, an NHANES exploratory notebook and a baseline model notebook.

github.com/kesserk/t2d-risk-predictor

Also in design: Diabetes Guidance RAG, a planned question-answering system over NICE NG28 and the ADA Standards of Care. The repository holds the design note only. Ingestion, retrieval, evaluation and serving are not built, so this is not a shipped system.
